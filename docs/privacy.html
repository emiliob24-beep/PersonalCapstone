Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Vehiko is a vehicle maintenance tracking app. This page explains what data the app handles and how. In short: there is no account, no server we run, and no data collection beyond what stays on your own device and your own private iCloud account.

No account required

Vehiko does not require you to sign up, log in, or create an account of any kind to use the app.

Data you enter

Information you add — vehicles, service records, scheduled maintenance, parts, insurance and registration details, and documents — is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.

iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, your data may sync privately to your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit, so it's available across your own devices. This sync goes directly between your device and your private iCloud storage — it is never sent to us or to any third party. We do not have access to it.

Receipt scanning

Vehiko can use your device's camera to scan receipts and auto-fill service or part details. This scanning and text recognition happens entirely on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Receipt photos are not uploaded anywhere or shared with us.

Notifications

Maintenance reminders and expiration alerts are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data passes through any external server we operate.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage tracking.
  • We don't use advertising or third-party ad networks.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone, because we never receive it in the first place.
  • We don't use any third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Children's privacy

Vehiko is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vehikoapp@gmail.com.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a vehicle?

Open the Garage tab and tap the "+" button, or tap "Add Vehicle" if it's your first one. You can fill in details manually or look up a vehicle automatically by entering its 17-character VIN.

How does receipt scanning work?

When adding a service record or a part, tap "Scan Receipt" to capture a photo of your receipt. Vehiko reads the text on-device and tries to pre-fill the cost, date, mileage, and shop for you — you can always review and edit before saving.

Is my data backed up?

If you're signed into iCloud on your device, your data syncs privately to your own iCloud account, so it's available if you get a new device. Nothing is sent to us.

Can I share a vehicle with a family member?

Family sharing is on the way in an upcoming update. Right now, each vehicle's records live with the person who added them.

Does Vehiko support Spanish?

Yes — go to the Settings tab and change Language to Español (or leave it on System to follow your device's language automatically).
